FAQ

Common questions

Clear answers about Certiva peptides, testing, ordering and shipping.

Read the full FAQ
What is the Certiva pen?

A pre-filled, multidose research pen. It arrives factory-sealed, so there is no powder to mix, measure or draw by hand.

Is every batch tested?

Yes. Every batch is independently tested by a third-party laboratory for purity by HPLC, with identity confirmed by mass spectrometry.

Where can I see the certificate for my batch?

On our certificates of analysis page, or by scanning the QR code on your pen. Choose your compound and open the report whose batch number matches the label.

Where are your peptides made?

Manufactured in Britain, with specifications stated on every product listing.

How are orders sent?

Orders are dispatched quickly in temperature-protected packaging and always come with tracking.

Are your peptides for human use?

No. All Certiva peptides are for laboratory in vitro research use only, and are not for human or animal consumption.

How do I get in touch?

Use the chat button on this page or our contact form, and the team will get back to you.
